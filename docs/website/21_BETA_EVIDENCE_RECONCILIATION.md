# Beta evidence reconciliation

Date: 30 October 2026

This note records the claim boundary used for the website's private-beta update. It does not approve partnership, customer, compliance, or performance claims.

## Current public stage

- QualiTracker is pre-revenue and is not currently selling the eQMS.
- The user states that controlled laboratory beta testing begins in mid-November 2026 with a small cohort.
- The eQMS is the first product-validation priority. QualiBOT is a separate educational and source-grounded assistant track.
- Beta participation is not a customer sale, recurring product use, accreditation outcome, or performance guarantee.

## Attached event evidence safe to summarize

The October 2026 MeLSAT Quality Excellence Challenge report records:

- 276 registration records;
- 154 completed attempts;
- participation from 166 facilities, 27 regions, and all five zones;
- an average satisfaction score of 4.35 out of 5 from 57 survey responses.

The report notes duplicate registrations and distinguishes registrations, completions, demonstrations, expressions of interest, contacts, and customers. The website therefore labels these as event participation and feedback—not product adoption or revenue.

## Claims intentionally withheld

- The number or identity of beta laboratories, because the supplied drafts conflict and the laboratories have not supplied public-name permission here.
- NPHL or any host laboratory as a customer, reference site, or approved partner.
- An ongoing MeLSAT partnership, signed MoU, CPD approval, or shared program beyond the completed conference challenge.
- Pricing, savings, clinical impact, accreditation results, compliance certification, paid customers, or recurring usage.

## Source conflict kept visible

The user states that five laboratories have committed teams and that beta testing starts in mid-November 2026. The 12 October strategy draft describes one November testing laboratory and a later three-to-five-lab pilot, while the 7 October product implementation plan says the pilot start, duration, and participating sites were still to be confirmed. Public copy therefore uses only "a small cohort of laboratories" and the user-confirmed mid-November timing.
